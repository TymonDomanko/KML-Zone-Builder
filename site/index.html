KML Zone Builder

Outline suburbs and postcodes, draw your own shapes, then download as KML.

Boundaries come from OpenStreetMap. Zoom the map to the area you want, then press “Show boundaries here”. What's available varies by country.

    Click areas on the map to add or remove them from the selected zone. Draw shapes with the tools on the map or in the zone.
